Platform Highlights

Built for citizens, officers and administrators

From grievance reporting to department-level analytics, CivicMind provides a complete smart governance workflow.

🧠

AI-Assisted Classification

Complaint text is analyzed to suggest issue category and route it to the relevant department automatically.

⚡

Priority Scoring

Complaints are prioritized based on severity, duplicate reports, public importance and complaint age.

🔁

Duplicate Detection

Repeated grievances in the same area are grouped to reduce clutter and improve faster resolution.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Admins can view complaint trends, department workload, hotspot areas and resolution performance.

🧾

Status Tracking

Citizens can monitor the complaint lifecycle from submission to assignment, progress and resolution.

🚨

Escalation Monitoring

Unresolved complaints are escalated automatically when they exceed resolution thresholds.

Modules

Three role-based portals in one platform

Citizen Portal

  • Register & Login
  • Submit grievance
  • Track complaint status
  • View complaint history
  • Support duplicate complaints

Officer Portal

  • View assigned complaints
  • Update progress and remarks
  • Filter by area, category, priority
  • Resolve or escalate issues
  • Track pending workload

Admin Portal

  • Manage users and officers
  • Assign/reassign complaints
  • Monitor analytics dashboard
  • Track department performance
  • Review duplicates and escalations
Workflow

How CivicMind works

1

Citizen submits grievance

Complaint includes title, description, category, location, severity and optional image.

2

System triages complaint

AI-assisted rules identify department, priority, duplicate possibility and escalation risk.

3

Officer handles complaint

Assigned officer reviews the complaint, updates status and records remarks.

4

Admin monitors system

Admins review trends, hotspots, overdue complaints and department-wise resolution rates.
